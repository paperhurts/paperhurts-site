  • 20 imports from forum sites a day, plus one check a day for new chapters on each forum story you already have.

A day runs from midnight to midnight UTC. A forum story's daily check covers Check for Updates, Re-fetch and Fetch the rest. Once it's used, Check for Updates skips that story until the next day, and Re-fetch or Fetch the rest uses one of your imports. A fetch that fails normally doesn't count.

These limits keep the service running for everyone. Reaching one stops new additions; it never removes anything you've already stored, and the page-image limit never cuts off a book you've started uploading.
