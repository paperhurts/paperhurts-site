Email

One address covers everything: support@paperhurts.dev

A subject line helps it reach the right pile:

  • Supporter membership: ask to hear when membership opens, or take your request back.
  • Refund: see the refund policy.
  • Privacy: a copy of your data, a correction, or deleting your account.
  • Author removal: you wrote a story and want it out of Paperhurts. Copyright and takedown says what to send.
  • Copyright notice: a formal notice from a rights holder; see copyright and takedown for what to include.
  • Security: please tell us about a security problem privately and give us time to fix it before you share it.

If you can't sign in, write from the address on your account so we can tell it's you.

Who you're writing to

Run a website? About our fetcher

If you've seen paperhurts-reader/1.0 in your server logs, that's us. When a signed-in reader asks Paperhurts to bring in a story from a website, our server fetches the pages they asked for, and only those. It goes at a gentle pace and backs off when a site refuses.

If you'd like it to stop visiting your site, or it's causing you trouble, email support@paperhurts.dev with "Fetcher" in the subject and your site's address. We'll sort it out.

We'll never ask for your password

Not for Paperhurts, and not for any other site. If a message asks for one in our name, it isn't from us.
