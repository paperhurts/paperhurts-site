Refund policy

Draft of November 2, 2026 · Effective [date]

In short

  • Full refund within 30 days of any payment. No questions asked.
  • If Paperhurts Reader shuts down, you get back the unused part of your year. The same goes if we close your account through no serious fault of yours, or you leave over a change to our terms.
  • Cancel any time, and you won't be charged again.

This policy covers paid memberships of Paperhurts Reader, sold by paperhurts LLC through our payment provider. Accounts themselves are free, and membership isn't open yet. This policy is part of our terms.

30 days, no questions asked

Within 30 days of any payment, whether it's your first payment or a yearly renewal, you can ask for a full refund of that payment. You don't need to give a reason. Once the refund goes through, your membership ends, and your account carries on as a free account.

After 30 days

You can cancel at any time, and you won't be charged again. You keep your membership until the end of the year you've paid for. Once 30 days have passed since a payment, we don't refund the rest of that year, except in the cases below.

If Paperhurts Reader shuts down

If we ever close the service, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before it closes, so you have time to export your stories. We'll refund the unused part of your current year, pro-rata, counted in days from the day the service closes. You don't need to ask: the refund goes to the payment method you used.

If we close your account

If we close your account for any reason other than a serious or repeated breach of the terms, we refund the unused part of your current year, pro-rata. If we close it because of such a breach (for example, under our repeat-infringer policy), we don't refund the remaining time, unless the law where you live requires it.

If we change our terms

If we make a change to our terms or policies that matters, we'll email you at least 30 days before it takes effect. If you don't accept it, you can delete your account before then, and we'll refund the unused part of your current year, pro-rata.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@paperhurts.dev with "Refund" in the subject. Write from the address you paid with, or include it in the message. [You can also use the link in your receipt from the payment provider.]

Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

If something's wrong with a charge, please write to us before disputing it with your bank. We'll sort it out quickly, and a refund reaches you faster than a dispute does.
