1. Who we are

Paperhurts Reader is operated by paperhurts LLC, a Florida limited liability company, which is responsible for your data. Write to us at support@paperhurts.dev. This policy covers the Paperhurts Reader app and this website.

2. What we store

Your account

Your email address, your username, and your password stored only as a one-way hash (we can't read it). Also when you joined, your account type, and your membership status.

Your library

The stories and files you import: their text, chapters, author's notes, title, author, summary, tags, cover, and the web address they came from. Pictures inside a story, which we download and keep with it where we can. For PDFs and comics, the page images. We keep what's in the file, not the original file itself.

Your reading

Where you are in each story, bookmarks, highlights (with a few words either side, so they stay in place) and notes, collections, ratings and reviews, and your settings, such as fonts, themes, sort order and the pronunciations you teach the narrator.

Import history

For imports that fail, the address and the error, so you can retry. For each story, the results of its last few update checks. To apply the daily limits, a count of today's forum imports, and which forum stories were checked today; earlier days' records are cleared the next time you fetch from a forum site.

Removal notes

If a story in your library is removed at its author's request, a note takes its place with the story's title, author, length, your progress and its dates, so it sits where the story was and each of your devices knows to delete its copy. Dismissing the note hides it; it's deleted with your account.

API tokens

If you create one, we store only a one-way hash of it, its label, and when it was last used.

Payments

If supporter membership opens and you join, our payment provider handles checkout and tells us your email address, country, plan, payment status and renewal date. We never see or store your card number.

Messages

Emails you send us, including requests to hear when supporter membership opens, and our replies.

Server logs

A record of requests to our server: the time, which part of the app was used, your account's internal id, and whether it worked. Import messages can include a story's title and web address. Our server sees your IP address to answer each request, and holds it briefly in memory to stop abuse such as password guessing, but our logs don't record it.

Error reports

If the app hits an error, it sends us the error message, a technical trace, which screen you were on, a random id for that visit, and whether you were using the iPhone app. Anything that looks like a password or token is removed first. These end up in our server logs.

3. What stays on your device

To work offline and remember your settings, the app keeps some data in your browser's storage on your device: copies of stories you've opened, your library list, your place in each story, your settings, and the sign-in token that keeps you logged in (in local storage, not a cookie). Narration diagnostics, if you ever look at them, stay on your device unless you choose to send them to us. Signing out removes your library from that device.

4. How we use it

  • To run Paperhurts for you: store your library, sync it to your devices, narrate it, and let you export it.
  • To send you emails about your account: password resets, renewal reminders, and important notices about the service, such as changes to these policies or a security problem. We may send important notices to every account.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To keep the service secure, find and fix bugs, and stop abuse.
  • To keep out works their authors have asked us to remove: we check the web address of each story added to a library (for an EPUB file, the address it records) against a list of those works. The list holds only those addresses and when each was added: not who asked, and not who had the work.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax records and responding to valid legal requests.

We don't send marketing emails. If you ask to hear when supporter membership opens, we send you that one email.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't show ads, and we don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone for advertising.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts, no fingerprinting and no cookies, on this website or in the app.
  • We don't use your library to train AI, and the app never sends it to any AI service.
  • Narration uses your device's own voices. Your stories aren't sent to any speech service.
  • We never ask for your password to any other site.

6. Who can see your library

Other readers can't. Libraries are private: there are no public profiles, pages or shared collections. Where the app offers it, you can choose to send a copy of a book to another reader; they get their own copy and see your username.

Our server stores your library's text and page images so your devices stay in sync, and in the app only your account can see them. The developer who runs Paperhurts can reach the database, but doesn't open libraries except to fix a problem you report, or when the law requires it. Acting on an author's request or a copyright notice means looking a work up by its web address, not reading libraries.

We'd disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it, and we'd tell you unless the law forbids that.

7. Who processes it, and where

A few companies process data for us, each to provide its part of the service. Google and our payment provider also handle it under their own privacy policies:

Hosting

DigitalOcean runs our server, in its San Francisco data centre in the United States. Your account and library live there.

Backups

Nightly backups are stored with Backblaze B2, in its US West region in the United States. They're encrypted on our server before they're sent, so Backblaze can't read them.

Email delivery

Resend sends the app's emails: password resets and notices about the service, never marketing. It sees your email address and the message.

Our mailbox

Namecheap, our domain registrar, forwards mail sent to support@paperhurts.dev, or any other @paperhurts.dev address, to a Gmail mailbox. Google holds the emails you send us there, under its own privacy policy.

Uptime, disk and backup alerts

Our server sends healthchecks.io short status messages: every five minutes, whether the app is answering and how full the disk is, and each night, whether the backup worked. They carry only sizes, counts and error messages about the server, never anything from your account or library. If a message reports a problem, or they stop arriving, the developer gets an email.

Payments

[payment provider] is our reseller and merchant of record. It collects your payment details directly and handles them under its own privacy policy.

This website

This site is served by GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no scripts.

Our server and its backups are in the United States. If you use Paperhurts from elsewhere, your data is transferred there.

8. Other websites

  • Fetching a story. When you ask the app to bring in a story from a website, our server usually fetches it, and that site sees our server, not you. In the iPhone app, some sites are fetched by your phone instead, so those sites see your phone's connection, as they would if you visited them.
  • Pictures in stories. Some stories include pictures hosted on other websites that we couldn't keep a copy of. When you read one, your browser loads the picture straight from that website, which can see your IP address. The app sends it no information about what you're reading.
  • Word lookup. The dictionary runs on our own server from an open dictionary. Your lookups don't go anywhere else.
  • Links. If you follow a link to another website, its own privacy policy applies.

9. How long we keep it

  • Your account and library: until you delete them.
  • Something you delete: removed from our live systems straight away, and from backups within 35 days as old backups are replaced.
  • Server logs and error reports: up to 30 days.
  • Requests to hear when membership opens: until we've emailed you that it's open, or until you ask us to remove yours, whichever comes first. The limit for emails below still applies.
  • Emails with us: as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than [2 years].
  • Payment records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the Account page in the app, or by emailing us from the address on the account. Deleting it removes your account and everything in it: every story and file, page images, your place in every story, bookmarks, highlights, notes, settings and API tokens. It's removed from our live systems straight away, and from backups within 35 days.

Our payment provider keeps its own records of past payments, as the law requires. If you want your emails with us deleted too, just ask.

If you'd like to keep your stories, export them first.

11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data (the app can also export your stories at any time);
  • correct something that's wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • stop or limit a particular use of it.

Email support@paperhurts.dev from the address on your account, and we'll reply within 30 days. You won't be treated differently for asking.

If you're in California: we don't sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined in California law. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority. [EU/UK details: lawyer to confirm.]

12. Security

Connections to Paperhurts are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Backups are encrypted before they leave our server. Only the developer who runs Paperhurts administers the server; DigitalOcean hosts it and Backblaze stores its backups. No service is perfectly secure, though. If data is ever exposed, we'll email every account without undue delay with what happened and what you can do, and we'll notify the authorities where the law requires.

13. Age

Paperhurts is for adults: you must be 18 or older to use it. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data.

14. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll email you at least 30 days before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
