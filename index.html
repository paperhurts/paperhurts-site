Your fic library, read aloud.

Keep your own copies of the stories you love, drop them into paperhurts reader, and listen while you drive, cook or fall asleep. Your library stays private, and you can take it with you any time.

How it works Free to use

How it works

  1. Keep your copies. Save the stories you love as EPUB files, with the download button many fiction sites offer or with free tools like FicHub and FanFicFare. The copies are yours.
  2. Drop them in. Add many files at once. Each story keeps its title, author and chapters. Word, Markdown, PDF and CBZ comic files work too.
  3. Listen. paperhurts reads aloud with your device's own voices, and the page follows along. Change the speed, set a sleep timer, and teach it how to say the names it gets wrong.
  4. Take it with you. Export a story as an EPUB whenever you like. Your copies never get locked in.

Your own writing, too

Write a chapter, hear it read aloud, fix it, then post it. Your ear catches the missing word and the sentence that runs on: the things your eye skips because it knows what you meant.

  • A chapter at a time. In the iPhone app, share a chapter file from wherever you write it. Two short lines at the top say which story and which chapter, and it goes into that story as that chapter.
  • Fix it and send it again. Share the revised file, choose Replace, and the new version takes the old one's place. Mark what to fix with highlights and notes while you listen.
  • Markdown, plain text or Word. The iPhone app's share sheet takes Markdown and text files. In any browser, the upload button takes those and Word files, Google Docs exports included.
  • Still private. Your drafts sit in your library only, like everything else in it.

Reading, your way

  • Themes, or your own colours Fonts, size and spacing too.
  • Reads offline Add it to your home screen and keep reading without a signal.
  • Your place, everywhere Pick up on any device where you left off.
  • Bookmarks, highlights and notes Plus a built-in dictionary.
  • Books and comics too PDFs, comics and manga sit beside your stories.
  • Runs in your browser On a phone, a tablet or a computer.

What we don't do

  • No ads. Not beside your stories, not anywhere.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts, on this site or in the app.
  • No sharing. Your library is private. We never publish it or sell it, and other readers can't see it.
  • No passwords for other sites. paperhurts never asks for your login to any site you read on.
  • No charge for stories. We don't sell stories or charge for access to any of them.

Built by one developer with AI coding help. The app never sends your library to any AI service, and narration uses your device's own voices.

Free to use

paperhurts reader is in a small invited beta, and it's free for as long as paperhurts runs. If it ever shuts down, every account gets at least 30 days' notice to export.

Your account comes with these limits, and paying never changes a limit:

  • Stories 1,000 in your library.
  • Page images 2 GB, for PDF and CBZ files.
  • Forum sites 20 imports a day, plus one check a day for new chapters on each forum story you already have.
