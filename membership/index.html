Free accounts and membership

Draft of November 2, 2026

In short

  • Paperhurts Reader is free for as long as Paperhurts runs.
  • Every account has the same limits, listed below.
  • If Paperhurts ever shuts down, every account gets at least 30 days' notice to export.
  • Supporter membership isn't open yet. Ask, and we'll tell you when it opens.

Free for as long as Paperhurts runs

Paperhurts Reader is in a small invited beta. Your account is free, and it stays free for as long as Paperhurts runs. We don't sell stories or charge for access to any of them, and you can export your stories at any time.

The same limits for every account

Every account has the same limits, so the server stays quick for everyone:

  • 1,000 stories in your library;
  • 2 GB of page images, for PDFs and comic files;
  • 20 imports from forum sites a day.

When you reach a limit, the app tells you. A limit only stops new additions: it never removes anything you've already stored.

If Paperhurts shuts down

If we ever decide to close Paperhurts, we'll email every account at least 30 days before it closes, so you have time to export your stories.

Supporter membership

Supporter membership isn't open yet. Before it opens, this page will say what it costs and what it includes, and nobody pays anything unless they choose to join.

Tell me when supporter membership opens: email support@paperhurts.dev with "Supporter membership" in the subject. [mailbox not set up yet]

We use your address only to tell you when membership opens, and you can ask us to remove it at any time. The privacy policy says how long we keep it.

Questions

Write to support@paperhurts.dev. The terms cover the rest.
