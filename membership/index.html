Free accounts and membership

Draft of November 4, 2026

In short

  • Paperhurts Reader is free for as long as Paperhurts runs.
  • Your account has the limits listed below, and paying never changes a limit.
  • If Paperhurts ever shuts down, every account gets at least 30 days' notice to export.
  • Supporter membership isn't open yet. Ask, and we'll tell you when it opens.

Free for as long as Paperhurts runs

Paperhurts Reader is in a small invited beta. Your account is free, and it stays free for as long as Paperhurts runs. We don't sell stories or charge for access to any of them, and you can export your stories at any time.

Your account's limits

Your account comes with these limits, so the server stays quick for everyone. Paying never changes a limit.

  • 1,000 stories in your library;
  • 2 GB of page images, for PDF and CBZ files;
  • 20 imports from forum sites a day, plus one check a day for new chapters on each forum story you already have.

A day runs from midnight to midnight UTC. A forum story's daily check covers Check for Updates, Re-fetch and Fetch the rest. Once it's used, Check for Updates skips that story until the next day, and Re-fetch or Fetch the rest uses one of your imports. A fetch that fails normally doesn't count.

When you reach a limit, the app tells you, and the upload screen shows how much of your page-image space you've used. A limit only stops new additions: it never removes anything you've already stored, and the page-image limit never cuts off a book you've started uploading.

If Paperhurts shuts down

If we ever decide to close Paperhurts, we'll email every account at least 30 days before it closes, so you have time to export your stories.

Supporter membership

Supporter membership isn't open yet. Before it opens, this page will say what it costs and what it includes, and nobody pays anything unless they choose to join.

Tell me when supporter membership opens: email support@paperhurts.dev with "Supporter membership" in the subject.

We use your address only to tell you when membership opens, and you can ask us to remove it at any time. The privacy policy says how long we keep it.
