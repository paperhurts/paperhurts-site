Founding seats

Draft of November 1, 2026

In short

  • $24.99 a year, and your price never goes up while you keep your seat.
  • Every paid feature, now and later, at no extra cost.
  • Reserve by email today. Nobody pays anything until payment opens.
  • Full refund within 30 days of any payment.

What a founding seat is

Paperhurts Reader is in a small invited beta, and it's free for the readers in it while the beta lasts. A founding seat is a paid membership for readers who want to keep Paperhurts running and growing. It costs $24.99 a year.

What's included

  • Everything Paperhurts Reader does today: importing your own files, reading, narration with your device's voices, offline reading, export, and your library on all your devices.
  • Every paid feature we add later, at no extra cost, for as long as you keep your seat.

What a seat never pays for is a story. Paperhurts doesn't sell stories or charge for access to any of them, and the price never depends on how much you store or how big your library is. Paying or not, you can export your stories any time.

Locked for life

Your price stays $24.99 a year for as long as your seat stays active, even if prices go up for everyone else. If you cancel, your seat ends when the year you paid for runs out. If you come back later, the founding price applies again only if founding seats are still open.

How to reserve

Email support@paperhurts.dev with "Founding seat" in the subject, from the address you'd like the payment link sent to. [mailbox not set up yet]

That's all. Reserving is free and doesn't commit you to anything. You don't need to reply to anything to keep your place, and you can ask us to remove your reservation at any time. We use your address only to write to you about your seat, and we never add it to a mailing list.

When payment opens

We'll email you a payment link. Checkout is run by [payment provider], our reseller and merchant of record: it takes the payment, handles sales tax, and is the name on your card statement. We never see or store your card number.

Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax or VAT may be added at checkout, depending on where you live.

Renewal and cancelling

A founding seat renews automatically each year at $24.99 until you cancel. We'll email you before each renewal. You can cancel any time from [the link in your receipt] or by emailing us. After you cancel, you keep your seat until the end of the year you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Refunds

You can get a full refund within 30 days of any payment, first or renewal, with no questions asked. If Paperhurts Reader ever shuts down, we refund the unused part of your year. The details are in the refund policy.

Questions

Write to support@paperhurts.dev. The terms cover the rest.
